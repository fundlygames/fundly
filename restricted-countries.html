3.1. These restrictions reflect international sanctions regimes, AML/CTF requirements, and elevated jurisdictional risk. They are standard practice across financial and evaluation platforms and are applied independently of, and in addition to, any restrictions enforced by our payment processor.

4.2. Attempting to bypass these restrictions — for example, by using a VPN, proxy, or false residency information — is a violation of our Terms and Conditions and will result in account suspension or termination.

4.3. If you are a national of a restricted jurisdiction but reside elsewhere, you may be considered for onboarding on providing valid, verifiable proof of residence in a permitted country.
