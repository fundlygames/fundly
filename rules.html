2. Phase 1 — Challenge

  • Time limit: 30 days
  • Profit target: +10 % of capital
  • Max. overall loss: −10 % of capital (static — the floor never moves)
  • Max. daily loss: −4 % of capital, resets at midnight UTC
  • Minimum 5 qualifying winning entries, each with a net profit of at least +0.5 % of capital
  • Permissible odds range: 1.00 to 8.00

3. Phase 2 — Verification

  • Time limit: 30 days
  • Profit target: +5 % of capital
  • Same max. overall loss, max. daily loss, qualifying-entry and permissible-odds rules as Phase 1

4. Phase 3 — Fundly Partner (Partner Account)

  • Performance split: you keep 80 % of the performance reward
  • No time limit
  • Max. overall loss: −10 % (trailing from your highest recorded balance)
  • Max. daily loss: −4 % of capital, resets at midnight UTC

4.1 Maximum Entry Risk Limit

The maximum simulated risk per single entry is 1.5 % of account capital in every phase. This applies regardless of odds or sport, and is enforced automatically by the platform.

5. Contractor Payouts

  • A profit buffer of at least +5 % of capital is required before your first contractor payout request
  • Minimum 5 qualifying winning entries (≥ +0.5 % of capital each) required before contractor payout
  • Maximum $4,000 per contractor payout request; you receive your 80 % performance share
  • Identity verification (KYC) via Whop is required before the first contractor payout
  • Approved contractor payouts are settled within 48 hours

6. Forbidden Strategies

  • Arbitrage & mispricing exploitation across outcomes or platforms
  • Latency exploitation of stale data feeds
  • Multi-accounting or collusion between accounts (shared device, IP, or payment method used to coordinate opposing entries)
  • Any exploitation of technical errors, bugs, or mispriced simulation data

Accounts found in breach of these rules are terminated without contractor payout, in accordance with Section 6 of our Terms and Conditions.
