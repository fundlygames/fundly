Academy

Betting fundamentals for the Fundly Challenge

Learn the rules of the game before you bet
Five short reads — bankroll, odds, value, qualifying tickets and drawdown.

Bankroll management

  • Your bankroll is your challenge capital — treat it as a budget, not a number to double overnight.
  • Flat staking wins long-term: 1–2 % of capital per ticket. On Fundly the max stake is 1.5 % of capital anyway.
  • Never chase losses by raising stakes — that is how accounts hit the drawdown floor.

What odds mean

Decimal odds tell you two things: your return and the implied probability of the outcome.

Odds 1.50 → implied probability66.7 %
Odds 2.00 → implied probability50 %
Odds 4.00 → implied probability25 %
Return = stake × odds$10 × 2.00 = $20

Implied probability = 1 ÷ odds. Bookmakers build a margin into the odds, so the total of all outcomes is always over 100 %.

Value vs arbitrage

Value betting means betting when the odds are higher than the true probability — you have an edge and accept variance. Arbitrage (sure betting) means covering all outcomes across bookmakers for a guaranteed, risk-free profit.

Arbitrage and sure betting are FORBIDDEN on Fundly. Hedging outcomes for guaranteed profit breaks the challenge rules, gets flagged automatically and leads to a breach.

Qualifying tickets

  • To pass a phase (and before every payout) you need 5 qualifying tickets.
  • A qualifying ticket is a winning ticket with a net profit of at least +0.5 % of your capital.
  • The rule exists to prove consistent, real betting — not one lucky all-in.

Drawdown

Drawdown is how far your balance may fall before the challenge fails. In the Challenge phases the max. loss is a static −10 % of capital — a fixed floor. On the funded account it becomes trailing: the floor follows your highest reached balance.

Max. loss (static → trailing on funded) −10 % of capital
Max. daily loss (resets at midnight UTC) −4 % of capital

Example: with $25,000 capital the static floor is $22,500. On a funded account peaking at $27,000 the trailing floor moves to $24,300.

House rules in short

  1. Phase 1 target +10 %, Phase 2 target +5 % of capital, 30 days per phase.
  2. Max. loss −10 % (static in the Challenge, trailing on funded), max. daily loss −4 % of capital.
  3. Max. stake 1.5 % of capital per ticket.
  4. 5 qualifying tickets per phase and before every payout.
  5. No arbitrage, no sure betting, no abusive strategies — flagged tickets lead to a breach.
  6. Funded account: 80 % profit split, payouts within 48 hours of approval.
