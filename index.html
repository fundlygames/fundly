Frequently asked questions

Fundly is a prop firm for sports bettors. We give you capital from $2,000 to $100,000, you bet according to clear rules and keep 80 % of the profit. You never risk your own money — you only pay a one-time Challenge fee.

The Challenge has two phases. In the first you must reach a profit target of 10 % of capital, in the second 5 %, both within 30 days while respecting the static max. loss limit of 10 % of capital and the daily loss limit of 4 % of capital. In each phase you also need at least 5 winning tickets with a net profit of at least 0.5 % of capital. After passing both phases you get a funded account with no time limit and a trailing max. loss of 10 % from your highest balance.

You can bet on all sports with odds from 1.00 to 8.00. The maximum stake per ticket is 1.5 % of capital and in each phase you must have at least 5 winning tickets with a net profit of at least 0.5 % of capital, so the result never comes down to a single bet.

On a funded account you can request a payout after building a profit buffer of +5 % of capital and completing 5 qualifying tickets, up to $4,000 per payout. We approve requests within 24 hours and send the money to your account within 48 hours of approval.

You only lose the one-time Challenge fee, nothing more. You can buy a new Challenge anytime and try again — or restart cheaper: the reset fee is just 40 % of the package price.
